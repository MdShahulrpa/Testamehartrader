Before you call

Straight answers.

What do you buy?

Copper, iron and cast iron, steel and stainless steel, aluminium, brass and bronze, industrial batteries, e-waste and PCBs, and industrial equipment such as pumps, motors, gearboxes and heat exchangers. If you have something not listed, ask us.

How fast can you collect?

Our standard pickup turnaround is 24 hours. Send your location and material details and we will confirm a time.

How and when do I get paid?

Payment is made the same day by cash, RTGS, NEFT or UPI, after weighment.

Do you give proper documents?

Yes. We provide GST invoices, e-way bills, weight slips and material transfer documents.

Do you collect from our factory floor?

Yes. We offer doorstep and factory-floor pickup across Tamil Nadu.

What is the minimum quantity?

Tell us what you have and where. We will tell you whether and how we can collect it.
