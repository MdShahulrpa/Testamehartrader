Four steps. No surprises.

The same simple process whether it is 100 kg of copper or a full truckload of mixed scrap.

Share details

Call or WhatsApp with photos, material type, approximate quantity and pickup location.

Get the rate

We quote the best market rate for your material and agree a pickup time. Standard turnaround is 24 hours.

Pickup & weighment

Our team collects from your doorstep or factory floor. The material is weighed and a weight slip is issued.

Paid & documented

Payment the same day by cash, RTGS, NEFT or UPI, with GST invoice, e-way bill and material transfer documents.

Get ready for pickup

Help us quote faster.

  • Separate metals by type if you can (copper, aluminium, steel)
  • Send two or three clear photos of the pile
  • Tell us the access: ground level, dock, or upper floor
  • Keep your GST details ready for the invoice

Documents with every pickup

GST invoice
E-way bill
Weight slip
Material transfer document

Document requirements for specific items such as batteries and e-waste will be confirmed when we quote.

Questions

How payment and pickup work.

How is the rate decided?

We quote the best market rate for your material on the day of pickup. Rates move with the metals market, so we confirm the figure when you call.

Can I get payment before the truck leaves?

Payment is made the same day, after weighment, by cash, RTGS, NEFT or UPI.

Do you handle loading?

Our team collects from your doorstep or factory floor. Tell us the access conditions when you call so we send the right vehicle and crew.
