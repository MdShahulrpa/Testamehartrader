Industries we serve

Clear, documented scrap pickup for plants that generate scrap every week.

Automotive

Machining chips, rejected castings and forgings, die-cast aluminium, batteries and end-of-line equipment.

PCBs, rejected boards, servers and IT equipment, with e-waste handled by an e-waste certified process.
